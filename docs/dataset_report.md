## Data-quality findings

- Rows sharing a (service, document, timestamp): **26** (TikTok). These carry *different* texts captured at the same instant (regional / A-B variants), so they are kept as distinct versions.
- Exact duplicate rows (same timestamp and text): **6** — dropped on load.
- Versions whose full text is identical to an earlier version of the same document: **347** — this signals variants alternating back and forth (e.g. TikTok), which the change detector treats as reverts, not new changes.
- Suspiciously short captures (< 50 words): **28** (Instagram Privacy Policy 2025-05-21: 3 words; Instagram Privacy Policy 2025-05-25: 3 words; Instagram Privacy Policy 2025-05-26: 3 words; Instagram Privacy Policy 2025-06-03: 3 words; Instagram Privacy Policy 2025-06-13: 3 words; Instagram Privacy Policy 2025-06-14: 3 words; Instagram Privacy Policy 2025-06-19: 3 words; Instagram Privacy Policy 2025-06-21: 3 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-05-12: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-05-14: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-06-02: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-06-28: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-07-02: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-07-16: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-07-31: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-08-07: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-08-17: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-08-22: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-08-23: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-09-03: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-09-09: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-09-13: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2023-09-20: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2024-10-29: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2024-11-28: 24 words; Spotify Privacy Policy 2025-02-03: 24 words; Upscrolled Privacy Policy 2026-07-26: 12 words; Upscrolled Terms of Service 2026-07-26: 12 words). Treated as probable capture failures rather than real policy rewrites.
- Precomputed changes with ≤ 2 changed lines: **582** of 2,186; most are formatting / asset-URL noise (e.g. rotating image revision ids).
- Changes with no added text: 126; with no removed text: 154.
- `Twitter` (2022-09 → 2023-10) and `X` (2024-02 →) are separate services in the source. ClauseBerry keeps them separate (matching the archive) and notes the rename in the UI.
- `Mastodon` has a single version per document, so no change history is available.
- Coverage per service varies widely (Facebook: 603 versions; Moltbook: 4). The first time a clause is observed is therefore *not* necessarily when the practice began.

## How ClauseBerry uses the files

- `policy_versions.csv` is the source of truth: loaded into `policy_versions`, then diffed by `pipeline.clean_changes` with noise normalisation.
- `policy_changes.csv` is loaded into `source_changes` for provenance and cross-checking; ClauseBerry's own `policy_changes` table is recomputed from versions.
- `latest_policies.csv` is cross-checked against the latest version per document.
